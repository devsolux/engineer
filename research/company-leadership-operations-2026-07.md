# Company leadership and operating-system research

**Date**: 2026-07-16 · **Scope**: organizational context needed for product and company-system engineering

## Decision

Add one progressive-disclosure module that models the company as a socio-technical system and translates accepted executive policy into reliable controls, workflows, data, and evidence. Do not reproduce the private strategy skill's strategy, founder, hiring, capital-allocation, or general company-management ownership. Do not turn Engineer into legal, accounting, tax, or employment counsel.

## Coverage map

The minimum whole-company map spans board/owners; CEO; operations; technology and data; security and resilience; finance; product; marketing; sales, success, and partnerships; people and workplace relations; legal, privacy, contracts, records, and IP; procurement and vendors; customer support; risk, compliance, audit, and sustainability where material.

Titles are not universal contracts. Company stage, legal form, jurisdiction, regulated status, financing, workforce, and explicit delegation change the role map. The stable Engineer abstraction is accountable outcomes plus decision rights, evidence, controls, handoffs, metrics, exceptions, and escalation.

## Findings encoded

1. The G20/OECD governance principles separate strategic guidance and management monitoring from execution. They emphasize informed, good-faith board action, conflicts, internal control, risk, whistleblowing, stakeholder interests, and clear board-versus-management accountability.
2. COSO treats internal control as useful for operations and trustworthy information, not only financial-reporting compliance. The software consequence is objective-to-risk-to-control-to-evidence traceability across preventive, detective, and corrective controls.
3. The US DOJ evaluates whether compliance is well designed, effectively implemented, and effective in practice. Senior and middle management commitment, autonomy and resources, incentives, reporting/investigation, third parties, testing, and remediation must therefore be operational, not a policy checkbox.
4. NIST CSF 2.0 adds Govern as a top-level function and connects roles, policy, risk tolerance, oversight, enterprise risk, legal obligations, and supply-chain responsibilities. Security is an executive operating-system concern, not a CTO-only technical queue.
5. ISO 30414:2025 covers workforce composition, diversity, costs, productivity, safety and well-being, leadership/culture/engagement, compliance/ethics/relations, recruitment, mobility/succession, turnover, and skills. The revision adds auditable requirements and stronger data, privacy, and AI considerations.
6. ILO principles establish freedom of association and collective bargaining, freedom from forced and child labor and discrimination, and a safe and healthy workplace. Local law still controls concrete implementation.
7. EEOC and OSHA guidance shows why employee reporting needs accessible paths, confidentiality limits, anti-retaliation, prompt handling, reasonable access controls, understandable safety procedures, training, and records. These US sources are examples, not globally portable legal conclusions.
8. Google's team-effectiveness study identifies psychological safety, dependability, structure and clarity, meaning, and impact. Engineer must pair safety to speak with explicit accountability and should not reduce leadership to engagement-score optimization.
9. DORA finds leadership affects delivery by enabling technical and product capabilities; delivery counts alone are not leadership outcomes. Technology leadership systems should connect user value, delivery performance, reliability, culture, and organizational results.
10. SEC management-discussion guidance emphasizes material trends, uncertainty, liquidity, capital resources, critical estimates, and clear management perspective. It is a public-company disclosure source, but its materiality and narrative principles improve internal decision surfaces when not misrepresented as a private-company mandate.
11. FTC guidance requires truthful, non-deceptive, evidence-backed advertising and appropriate data security. Marketing systems need claim substantiation, disclosures, consent, privacy promises, and retained-value reconciliation rather than lead volume alone.
12. CLOC's legal-operations model covers business intelligence, financial/vendor/information/knowledge management, organization health, practice operations, programs, service delivery, strategy, technology, and training. Engineer may implement legal operations while reserving legal judgment to qualified counsel.

## Anti-overengineering decision

One module and one cross-domain eval are proportionate. Separate CEO, CTO, CFO, CMO, HR, and legal encyclopedias would duplicate the private strategy skill, age quickly, increase routing ambiguity, and imply false professional authority. Expand later only when captured tasks expose a stable, material implementation gap.
