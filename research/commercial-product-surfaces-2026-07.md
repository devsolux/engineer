# Commercial and product surfaces review

**Reviewed**: 2026-07-15

## Scope and decision

The existing conversion, design, analytics, GTM, product-marketing, billing, and business-engineering
modules covered the main route. The useful gap was stronger surface-specific evidence and a clearer
boundary between business decisions and their implementation.

Engineer now owns:

- landing and sales page message match, truthful material terms, qualified-outcome measurement, and
  field performance by page and segment;
- portfolio and case-study provenance, contribution boundaries, crawlable progressive enhancement,
  and protected client information;
- decision-oriented dashboards with metric semantics, freshness, reconciliation, uncertainty, and
  separate executive, operational, analytical, and incident jobs;
- product-management evidence from requirement through cohort release, impact review, iteration, or
  kill decision;
- sales, partner, RevOps, and company-policy workflows as reliable state machines, integrations,
  permissions, audit trails, and reconciled evidence.

The private strategy skill remains the owner of positioning choice, business strategy, organization and leadership,
capital allocation, hiring, and founder judgment. Engineer implements accepted decisions.

## Evidence retained

- W3C form guidance favors required-only inputs, explicit labels and instructions, error recovery,
  logical steps, progress, and reasonable time behavior.
- The FTC identifies disguised ads, fake scarcity, hidden fees and terms, obstructed cancellation,
  and manipulative privacy choices as deceptive patterns. Conversion never overrides autonomy.
- Chrome Lighthouse combines performance, accessibility, best-practice, and SEO diagnostics, but a
  lab score is not field behavior or business causality.
- web.dev case studies use field monitoring and controlled experiments to connect Core Web Vitals
  work to business outcomes; results from one company are not universal effect sizes.
- GOV.UK service measurement starts with purpose, hypotheses, sources, context, segments, trends,
  baselines, user research, and iteration; dashboards support decisions rather than replace analysis.
- GitLab's published product flow connects release, UX/functional/technical/customer assessment,
  success metrics, iteration, and abandonment when the expected outcome does not materialize.
- Stripe's checkout guidance reinforces total-cost clarity, short recoverable forms, guest checkout,
  suitable payment methods, mobile operation, confirmation, and ongoing cross-device testing.
